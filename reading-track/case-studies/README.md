# Case Studies

Worked walkthroughs of specific hardware articles, decoding terms inline against the reading-track topic files. The goal: by the end of three of these, you can read a new article cold.

Each case study follows the same structure:

1. **The article.** A 1-paragraph summary of what it claims.
2. **Prerequisite topics from the reading track.** Read these first.
3. **The terms.** Each major term is decoded — what it means, where in the architecture it lives, why the article cares.
4. **The argument.** The article's claim re-stated in your own words once you have the vocabulary.
5. **What's speculative vs what's whitepaper.** Honest separation of confirmed facts from analyst inference.
6. **Further reading.** Where to go to verify or deepen.
